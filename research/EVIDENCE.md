# Research evidence

Reference sites: pk.sapphireonline.pk, limelight.pk, urge.pk. Captured: `15 November 2026`, desktop browser.
Observations are facts seen on screen. Consequences are labelled as assumptions.

## Problem A: Size is not usable on listings
Task: find a piece available in my size.
**Observed**
- URG (`urge.pk/collections/new-arrivals`): the filter panel has Sort By, Availability and Price. No size option. `screenshots/urg-A-filter-panel.png`
- Limelight (`limelight.pk/collections/pret`): 574 products; cards show image, name and price only. `screenshots/limelight-A-listing.png`

**Possible consequence (assumption):** shoppers open several pages to check a size.

**Loom & Lane response:** size chips on cards and a size filter.

## Problem C: Fees and conditions appear late
Task: find the total I will pay.
**Observed**
- URG basket: Rs. 11,060, no shipping line seen. `screenshots/urg-C-basket.png`
- URG checkout: Shipping Rs. 99, total Rs. 11,159. `screenshots/urg-C-checkout.png`
- URG checkout policy popup: opened on "Shipping" but shows the exchange policy (customer pays courier both ways). `screenshots/urg-C-policy.png`
- Sapphire checkout: Rs. 4,990 item, Shipping Rs. 249, "FBR POS" Rs. 1, total Rs. 5,240; non-exchange and 5-7 day notes in the summary. `screenshots/sapphire-C-checkout.png`
- I could not find a free-shipping rule on the Sapphire product page or bag.

**Possible consequence (assumption):** an unexpected total may make shoppers abandon checkout.

**Loom & Lane response:** fee and free-delivery threshold shown in the bag, the product page and the announcement bar.

## Problem D: A display preference is not remembered
Task: browse in compact card view.
**Observed:** 
- Chose small cards on URG, refreshed, and the page returned to large cards. `screenshots/urg-D-small.png`, `screenshots/urg-D-after-refresh.png`

**Possible consequence (assumption):** repeated re-selection on every visit. Low severity.

**Loom & Lane response:** the choice is saved in localStorage.
